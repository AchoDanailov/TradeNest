# TradeNest - Software Requirements Specification Overview

TradeNest is a comprehensive, all-in-one online marketplace platform designed to facilitate the buying and selling of a diverse range of products. This document provides a high-level overview of the system's objectives, its users, and the core functional scope.

## Project Purpose
The primary goal of TradeNest is to provide a secure and user-friendly environment where individuals can list their products for sale and purchase items from other community members. The system manages the entire lifecycle of a product listing, from creation and administrative approval to final sale and delivery tracking.

## User Roles
TradeNest simplifies the user experience by defining two primary roles:

1.  **User**: A standard registered member of the platform. A User has the dual capability to:
    *   **Buy**: Browse the catalog, manage a shopping cart, and place orders.
    *   **Sell**: Create product listings, manage their inventory, and track sales performance.
2.  **Administrator**: A specialized role responsible for platform oversight, including:
    *   Managing product categories.
    *   Reviewing and approving/rejecting product listings.
    *   Overseeing user accounts and platform integrity.

## Core Objectives
*   **Trust and Quality**: Implement a rigorous approval workflow to ensure all public listings meet platform standards.
*   **Seamless Commerce**: Provide a persistent shopping cart and robust checkout process with real-time stock validation.
*   **Operational Transparency**: Offer sellers detailed statistics on their sales and product performance.
*   **Scalability**: Maintain a modular architecture that allows the platform to grow in features and user base.
